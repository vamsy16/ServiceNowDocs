How this was produced

Grounded in the docs
Every capability, navigation path and entitlement statement traces to a named topic in the Australia-release Impact documentation (listed in Appendix E of the brief). Nothing is inferred from marketing material, and entitlement-dependent figures are marked verify.
One model, three outputs
The deck's 31 slides and speaker notes come from a single content model, rendered to PowerPoint, PDF and this HTML viewer — so the versions cannot drift apart.
